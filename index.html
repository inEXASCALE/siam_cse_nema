The minisymposium

The arithmetic beneath the algorithm.

Modern GPUs and specialized accelerators offer a growing range of low precision floating point and integer formats. That creates opportunities for faster scientific computing, alongside new questions about accuracy, stability, and reproducibility.

This session brings together work on arithmetic emulation, mixed precision algorithms, error analysis, matrix computations, and emerging numerical standards.
